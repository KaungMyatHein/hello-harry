01 · The Problem

"Show me past 2 weeks." The dashboard couldn't.

A Myanmar live-seller has one question every Sunday: what did I sell in the past 2 weeks? One sentence. Clear intent.

The CRM dashboard turned that one sentence into a five-step hunt — and most sellers gave up halfway.

  1. Open — Orders tab, buried two levels deep
  2. Filter — no preset; pick dates manually
  3. Translate — every label in English
  4. Scroll — 600 rows, no summary at the top
  5. Give up — rebuild in Excel by hand
⚠️
The seller already knew the question. The system made them prove it.

04 · Tool-calling

LLM as classifier. Tools as verbs.

Gemini 2.5 Flash picks 1–6 tools per turn from a 16-verb vocabulary. The server runs them, scoped to businessId.

🔧
A tool is a verb the AI is allowed to say. Read · write · modify · delete. Each one returns a card schema the frontend renders inline.

05 · Bilingual

Myanmar isn't English in different glyphs.

Postpositions, subject markers, stacked digits. Each needs first-class support, not retrofits.

English
"Show me past 2 weeks"
→ list-orders · range=14d
Myanmar
"ပြီးခဲ့တဲ့ ၂ ပတ်"
→ list-orders · range=14d
Mixed
"customer Hnin past orders"
→ search-customers + list-orders

Reflection

What I learned
  1. A command palette isn't a UI pattern. It's a contract. The seller speaks naturally; the system promises to meet them there.
  2. Don't pretend Myanmar is "English with different glyphs." Postpositions, subject markers, stacked digits need first-class support.
  3. Matching intent is half the problem. Matching the user's expectation of the result is the other half.
  4. Regex is a feature, not a fallback. Predictable behavior is itself a way of reducing what the user has to learn.
