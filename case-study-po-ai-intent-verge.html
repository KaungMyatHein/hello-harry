01 · The Problem

"Show me past 2 weeks." The dashboard couldn't.

A Myanmar live-seller has one question every Sunday: what did I sell in the past 2 weeks? One sentence. Clear intent.

The CRM dashboard turned that one sentence into a five-step hunt. Open. Filter. Translate. Scroll. Give up. Rebuild in Excel.

"The seller already knew the question. The system made them prove it."
Field observation · 5 live sessions

04 · Tool-calling

LLM as classifier. Tools as verbs.

Gemini 2.5 Flash picks 1–6 tools per turn from a 16-verb vocabulary. The server runs them, scoped to businessId. A tool is a verb the AI is allowed to say.

05 · Bilingual

Myanmar Isn't English in different glyphs.

Postpositions, subject markers, stacked digits. First-class support — not retrofits.

Reflection

What I learned.

01
A command palette isn't a UI pattern. It's a contract.

The seller speaks naturally; the system promises to meet them there.

02
Myanmar isn't English in different glyphs.

Postpositions and stacked digits need first-class support, not retrofits.

03
Matching intent is half the problem.

Matching the user's expectation of the result is the other half.

04
Regex is a feature, not a fallback.

Predictable behavior reduces what users learn. LLM is the rare path.
