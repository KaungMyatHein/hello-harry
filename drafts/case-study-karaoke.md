## 01 — The Problem

*Karaoke bars closed. Digital alternatives were clunky, region-locked, or missing the point entirely.*

> "I miss singing with friends... lockdown is so boring. I need social entertainment at home."

Myanmar had no localized karaoke app. Users were stuck with foreign apps that lacked Myanmar songs, couldn't accept local payments, and felt nothing like the bar experience they craved.

**But what exactly were they missing?**

---

## 02 — What 20 Users Told Us

*We interviewed karaoke enthusiasts, casual singers, and music lovers across 4 research phases.*

Three patterns emerged:

- **It's social, not solo.** Users went to bars for the atmosphere — not just the mic. Even those with home karaoke machines still went out.
- **Singing is stress relief.** 80% described it as therapeutic. This isn't entertainment — it's emotional regulation.
- **Subscriptions feel natural.** 80% already use Netflix. No education needed — just demonstrate value.

**So how do we design for emotion — not just function?**

---

## 03 — Three Numbers That Shaped Everything

- **65%** search by artist first — so we made it the default. Friction dropped immediately.
- **70%** have a favourite genre — clear signal for personalization.
- **85%** use KBZ Pay — Myanmar's dominant payment method. Skip it and you lose the market.

**We had the data. Now we needed to decide what to build — and what to cut.**

---

## 04 — Competitors: Patterns, Not Blueprints

- **Smule** — Social rooms drive engagement. Community is the product.
- **Joox** — Music library is table stakes. Without catalogue depth, nothing else matters.
- **Star Maker** — Emotion beats perfection. Polish the feeling, not the pitch.

We adapted their insights to a resource-constrained, localized MVP.

**The hardest part? Deciding what NOT to build.**

---

## 05 — MVP Scope: Keep, Cut, Defer

| Keep | Cut | Future |
|------|-----|--------|
| Basic karaoke + artist search | Social rooms | Multiplayer |
| Subscription model + KBZ Pay | Video recording | Community challenges |
| Song feedback | Advanced recommendations | AR effects, Live streaming |

**With scope locked, we moved to flows and wireframes.**

## 09 — What This Project Taught Me

**01 — Defaults compound.** Artist search first — one decision that cascaded through the entire feature set and freed up cognitive load across millions of taps.

**02 — Local payment = local product.** KBZ Pay wasn't a nice-to-have. It was the conversion gateway. In emerging markets, payment choice is make-or-break.

**03 — Constraints breed clarity.** Tight timeline forced MVP rigor. The features we cut taught us as much as the ones we built.
