01 · Problem

Sellers type with one hand, hold inventory with the other.

In 21 contextual interviews across Yangon and Mandalay, every seller had the same blocker: switching between Messenger chats, the seller app, and a paper notebook. They wanted to say it once and have the system file the order, deduct stock, print a receipt, and tag the buyer.

Friction · A
App-switching tax
Average seller toggled between 3 apps to file a single live-stream order, while their hands held product on camera.
Friction · B
Number drift
Prices and stock quantities mistyped one digit off. Mistakes compounded into refund threads at the end of every live.
Friction · C
Paper-to-app gap
Orders scribbled on paper during peak got re-keyed hours later — often wrong, sometimes lost.
02 · Research

21 interviews. 4 archetypes. 1 shared verb-noun grammar.

I shadowed sellers for 2–3 hours each. The breakthrough: regardless of category — beauty, food, electronics, fashion — sellers spoke in the same shape. Verb + product + modifier. The grammar was already there. We just had to listen.

"ငါက လက်က ထုပ်နေတယ်၊ ပါးစပ်နဲ့ပဲ ပြောချင်တာ။ App က နားလည်ပေးရင် တော်လောက်ပါပြီ။"

— Seller A · Yangon · March 14, 2026
03 · Intent Mapping

Utterance to tool-call: a 6-phase deterministic chain.

No LLM gets to invent verbs. The model selects from a closed set of 28 named tools. Each tool has a JSON schema. The model fills slots; the runtime validates and executes.

05 · Bilingual

Burmese-first, English-tolerant, code-switched by default.

Real sellers mix languages every sentence. We trained the router on 4,200 code-switched utterances. Zero translation step.

my · Pure Burmese
"ဆေး နှစ်ဖူး ထည့်ပေး၊ ဈေး နှစ်ထောင်"
order.create(item=ဆေး, qty=2, price=2000)
en · Pure English
"add two units, price two thousand"
order.create(qty=2, price=2000, item=<prompt>)
mix · Code-switched
"stock ၃ ဖူး deduct, receipt print ပေး"
stock.deduct(qty=3) → receipt.print

∞ · Reflection

Four lessons I'll carry into the next AI feature.

01
Tools, not freeform text.
Closing the action set to 28 tools killed an entire class of hallucination error.
02
Latency is the feature.
Below 50ms feels like causation. Above 200ms feels like a server. We optimized phase-by-phase.
03
Bilingual is one language.
Treating code-switching as a first-class input cut router complexity in half.
04
The card is the truth.
Voice in, card out. The seller never re-listens — they glance, tap, move on.
