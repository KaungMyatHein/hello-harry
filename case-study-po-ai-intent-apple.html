01 — The Problem

"Show me past 2 weeks." The dashboard couldn't.

A Myanmar live-seller has one question every Sunday: what did I sell in the past 2 weeks? One sentence. Clear intent.

The CRM dashboard turned that one sentence into a five-step hunt — and most sellers gave up halfway. Open. Filter. Translate. Scroll. Give up. Rebuild in Excel by hand.

"The seller already knew the question. The system made them prove it."
Field observation · 5 live sessions

Reflection

What I learned.

01
A command palette isn't a UI pattern. It's a contract.

The seller speaks naturally; the system promises to meet them there.

02
Don't pretend Myanmar is "English with different glyphs."

Postpositions and stacked digits need first-class support — not retrofits.

03
Matching intent is half the problem.

Matching the user's expectation of the result is the other half. Ranking ≠ enumeration.

04
Regex is a feature, not a fallback.

Predictable behavior reduces what users learn. The LLM is the rare path.
