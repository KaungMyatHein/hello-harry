# Script: Golden Paths — "Prototypes that never sleep"

**Duration:** ~45 seconds (~110 words)
**Word count:** 110

## Script

Most designers know they should test their designs with real users, but finding five testers takes half a day — by the time you're done, the team has already shipped. So I built a tool where AI characters walk through your design like real users would: confused, distracted, in a hurry. They write back what tripped them up, in under a minute.

I checked it the hard way. I ran three usability sessions both AI and real users. The AI caught 9 of the 11 problems real users hit. The 2 it missed were emotional reactions — that's exactly where humans should still do the work.

It's live as a free tool today, and ten designer friends are early adopters.

If you're hiring me for AI that helps designers ship faster, this is the project.
