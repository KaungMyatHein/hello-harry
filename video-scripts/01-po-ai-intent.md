# Script: Po AI Intent Palette — "Speak, don't click"

**Duration:** ~40 seconds (~95 words)
**Word count:** 95

## Script

Po AI is a search tool I designed for Myanmar live-commerce sellers — the people running live shopping streams on Facebook. Their CRM dashboard made one simple question — "what did I sell this week?" — into a five-step search.

So I replaced it. You just type or say what you want, in English or Burmese, and the app does the work. I built it after sitting with five sellers during their real live streams. They didn't want better menus. They wanted to talk to the app like a person.

I was the sole designer, working with one backend engineer and the founder. 21 of those shortcuts are live today.

If you're hiring me to make complicated software feel simple, this is the project to look at.

## Speaker notes
- Warm, conversational tone — you're explaining it to a non-designer friend
- "Five-step search" — say it like the absurd specific it is
- "In English or Burmese" — say it naturally, no over-emphasis
- "Talk to the app like a person" — slight smile in the voice
- "21 of those shortcuts are live today." — small pause before, let the number sit
- Closing pitch — say it like an offer, not a tagline
