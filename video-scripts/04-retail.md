# Script: Retail Returns — "From feedback to 427% ROI"

**Duration:** ~45 seconds (~110 words)
**Word count:** 110

## Script

At Raytail — a retail chain in the Maldives — a colleague mentioned at a workshop that cashiers were struggling with returns, and nobody knew why. So I went to the shop floor and watched. The return button was buried so deep that cashiers were asking a manager every time. Customers stood at the counter, waiting.

I asked cashiers and business leaders to rank features by importance. Cashiers put returns first. The business team put it near the bottom — they'd never measured the cost.

I rebuilt the flow. Returns went from 60 seconds to under 30. Finance confirmed the design paid back four times over — a 427% return.

If you're hiring me for design that makes real money, this is the project.
