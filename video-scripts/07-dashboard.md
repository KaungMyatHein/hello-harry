# Script: Dashboard — "Daily ritual to 104% ROI."

**Duration:** ~45 seconds (~110 words)
**Word count:** 110

## Script

Every morning at TreeDots, the operations manager spent 30 minutes copying numbers from three different tools into a spreadsheet. By the time she was done, the team was already making decisions on yesterday's numbers.

The data wasn't missing. It was just scattered across three places. So I built one dashboard that pulls all of it together in real time. Thirty minutes became zero.

Finance confirmed the design paid for itself in six months and saved 125 hours a year. The number-card I designed got adopted into the company's design system, too.

If you're hiring me to bring scattered tools into one place and prove the design pays for itself, this is the project.
