01 · Problem

Sellers type with one hand, hold inventory with the other.

In 21 contextual interviews across Yangon and Mandalay, every seller had the same blocker: switching between Messenger chats, the seller app, and a paper notebook. They wanted to say it once and have the system file the order, deduct stock, print a receipt, and tag the buyer.

02 · Research

21 interviews. 4 archetypes. 1 shared verb-noun grammar.

I shadowed sellers for 2–3 hours each. The breakthrough: regardless of category — beauty, food, electronics, fashion — sellers spoke in the same shape. Verb + product + modifier. The grammar was already there. We just had to listen.

A
The Solo Shop Owner
Runs a Facebook beauty page. Live-sells 4 nights/week. Wants to whisper orders while on camera.
B
The Family Operation
3 family members share one phone. Lots of voice notes traded across rooms.
C
The Reseller
Orders from 3 suppliers, resells to 200 retail buyers. Needs voice-driven re-orders fast.

"ငါက လက်က ထုပ်နေတယ်၊ ပါးစပ်နဲ့ပဲ ပြောချင်တာ။ App က နားလည်ပေးရင် တော်လောက်ပါပြီ။"

— Seller A · Yangon · Mar 14
03 · Intent Mapping

From utterance to tool-call: a 6-phase deterministic chain.

No LLM gets to invent verbs. The model selects from a closed set of 28 named tools. Each tool has a JSON schema. The model fills slots; the runtime validates and executes. If a slot is missing, the assistant asks one targeted question.

05 · Bilingual

Burmese-first, English-tolerant, code-switched by default.

Real sellers mix languages every sentence. We trained the router on 4,200 code-switched utterances from the 21 interviews + diary studies. Zero translation step. The router operates on tokens, not language tags.

06 · Inline Confirmation Cards

No chat bubbles. Just compact, editable result cards.

Every tool result renders as a card the seller can tap to amend. Numbers are big, status is colored, the primary action is always reversible. The card is the source of truth — the voice is the input method.

∞ · Reflection

Four lessons I'll carry into the next AI feature.

01
Tools, not freeform text.
The moment we let the model speak unstructured, hallucination crept in. Closing the action set to 28 tools killed an entire class of error.
02
Latency is the feature.
Below 50ms feels like causation. Above 200ms feels like a server. We optimized phase-by-phase, not end-to-end.
03
Bilingual is one language.
Treating code-switching as a first-class input — not a translation step — cut router complexity in half.
04
The card is the truth.
Voice in, card out. The seller never re-listens. They glance, tap, move on. The interface earns the trust the voice can't.
